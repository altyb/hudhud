# Changelog

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-12)
New logo and icon. One theme now, Material You style with square keys: ten surfaces and eleven accents, any combination, with a live preview. Settings is a short list with a guide on each page. A language switch on the symbols page (swipe the space bar), more symbols on long-press, and a % key. The keyboard keeps clear of the gesture bar. The three dots are a settings gear.

## 1.3.1 (2026-10-31)
Offensive words and slurs are no longer suggested, in any language. You can still type them, and words you have taught the keyboard keep working. Autocorrect may change one the first time, as it does with any word the keyboard does not know.

## 1.3.0 (2026-10-31)
New app ID, `dev.altayeb.hudhud`. This installs as a new keyboard: save a backup in the old app, restore it here, then turn the new keyboard on.

## 1.2.1 (2026-10-31)
The question mark is on the same key in every language. Long-press offers both ? and ؟ everywhere. Arabic punctuation on the Arabic symbols page. French gets its ? hint.

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-31)
French, Spanish, German, Portuguese and Russian. All Unicode emoji with skin tones. Edit buttons. Voice hand-off to a voice keyboard you installed. Backup and restore. Light theme and accent colours. Split layout on wide screens. Copied images in clipboard history. Licenses screen and privacy policy. Landscape keys now fit the screen.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-31)
New name. Glide typing, cursor and delete swipes, one-handed mode, emoji search.

## 1.0.2 (2026-10-29)
New logo with adaptive and themed icons.

## 1.0.1 (2026-10-29)
Next-word predictions. Learned words reach the suggestion strip.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-29)
First release.
